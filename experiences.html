Itchetucknee Springs River Adventure

Float, tube, or kayak down miles of crystal-clear, spring-fed river winding through shaded hammocks and wildlife-rich wilderness. Itchetucknee Springs State Park is one of Florida's most beloved natural playgrounds, just a short drive from The Jungle Cat — bring water shoes and plan to lose track of time.
